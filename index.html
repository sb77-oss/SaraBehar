    On taste

    I trained as a historian, which is the discipline of deciding what matters and what story to tell.

    With enough time and money, anyone can generate as much content as they like. But without taste, curation and editing, it will likely remain flat, generic and forgettable.

    You can't put just anyone at the end of a machine and expect good work. Someone still has to know what to ask for, what to keep and what to cut. I use these tools every day, which is exactly how I know what they can't do on their own.

    I've always been drawn to the act of choosing. Give me a free morning on the beach, and I'll spend hours picking out stones and shells. My favorite part is selecting who each one should go to. I run my trivia quizzes the same way, collecting and shaping knowledge my players will love.

    It's the same instinct in all my work. Anyone can produce more. What I bring is the judgment to choose what's worth keeping, and the care to shape it for the person on the other end.
