    On taste

    I trained as a historian, which is the discipline of deciding what matters and what story to tell.

    With enough time and money, anyone can generate as much content as they like. That doesn't make them writers. Without taste, curation and editing, you get what most of the internet already sounds like: flat, generic and forgettable.

    AI hasn't changed that. It has raised the stakes. You can't put just anyone at the end of the machine and expect good work. Someone still has to know what to ask for, what to keep and what to cut.

    Most good writers won't tell you they love writing. I love the moment I find the right word. Give me a free morning on the beach and I'll spend it choosing stones and shells. It's the same instinct.

    It's also why my trivia night works. Every question is chosen, cut and checked. I have a regular crowd of 40, and at the last one 70 people came and I turned away at least 80 more.
